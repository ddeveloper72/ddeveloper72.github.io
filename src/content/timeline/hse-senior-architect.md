Leading technical architecture for Ireland's participation in the European Health Data Space (EHDS), including operational MyHealth@EU cross-border services and contribution to European standards development through the Xt-EHR project.

## Key Responsibilities

- Technical design and governance for MyHealth@EU Patient Summary implementation
- Contributing to **Xt-EHR Project** (European Health Data Space) across multiple work packages:
  - WP6.1: Patient Summaries towards EHDS
  - WP6.2: Electronic Prescriptions and Dispensations  
  - WP7.1: Laboratory Results and Reports
  - WP7.2: Medical Imaging and Reports
- Architecture and standards leadership for FHIR adoption across HSE
- API strategy, design patterns, and developer experience
- Testing and evaluation of national infrastructure services (NMPC API, Central Terminology Server)
- Security architecture for healthcare data exchange
- Technical representation in European eHealth infrastructure projects and standards development

## Notable Projects

- **MyHealth@EU National Contact Point**: Technical architecture for Ireland's cross-border healthcare data exchange, enabling Patient Summary and ePrescription services across EU member states
- **Xt-EHR Project**: European Health Data Space initiative - developing FHIR implementation guides for Patient Summaries, ePrescription/eDispensation, Laboratory Reports, and Medical Imaging (WP6.1, 6.2, 7.1, 7.2)
- **National Medicinal Product Catalogue (NMPC) API**: Testing and evaluation of national medication management infrastructure - standardized medication codes and terminology using SNOMED CT for primary and secondary care
- **Central Terminology Server (CTS)**: Evaluation of FHIR terminology service providing SNOMED CT and Irish clinical reference sets for national healthcare systems
- **EHDS Implementation**: Preparing Ireland for European Health Data Space Regulation compliance (March 2029 deadline)
- **FHIR Integration Platform**: Modern API infrastructure for healthcare applications
- **Standards Documentation**: Comprehensive FHIR implementation guides for Irish healthcare

## Technical Impact

Established Ireland as an active participant in European health data exchange through MyHealth@EU services. Contributing to pan-European standards development through Xt-EHR and HL7 Europe working groups, ensuring Irish requirements are represented in EHDS specifications and international medication standards. Established FHIR as the primary integration standard for new HSE systems, improving interoperability, reducing integration costs, and enabling modern healthcare applications.
